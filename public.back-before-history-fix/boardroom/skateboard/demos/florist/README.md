Refinement of previous architectural site. No redesign of architecture, layout, scrolling, typography, or colour palette. Conversion to luxury florist.

- Layer 2: Fixed centre logo (ASH & OAK) opacity 0.09, never animates

1. **Industry Conversion:** All copy, titles, metadata converted to floral design. Furniture removed.
2. **Collections:** Six genuinely distinct botanical arrangements with editorial images:

3. **Hero:** Single white rose on warm plaster, extreme negative space
4. **Rail Redesign:** Permanent solid architectural spine, 96px, deep navy matte, warm ivory typography only ASH & OAK stacked vertically near top. No dots, no indicators, no cut-outs. Subtle shadow. Feels like building, not UI.
5. **Logo Reveal:** 78vh transparent full-width opening halfway through Collections. Foreground stops. Fixed navy + fixed logo visible. No animation, fade, scale. Calm, inevitable.
6. **Navigation:** Retained
7. **Enquiry:** Options replaced with Wedding, Event Styling, Corporate Flowers, Sympathy Flowers, Gift Bouquet, Other. Underlined inputs retained.
8. **Success:** Two-tone blended navy/ivory background (radial blend), large logo, elegant thank-you, back button to top.
